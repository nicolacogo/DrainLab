About DrainLab Tools

DrainLab Tools is a free online calculator for preliminary drainage design. Click a location in England or Wales, draw a catchment, and get FSR (Wallingford Procedure) rainfall intensities for any duration and return period, together with a Rational Method peak runoff estimate.

It was created by a UK drainage engineer to speed up the early stages of drainage design, in a tool that is quick, transparent and free to use. Every calculation step is shown so you can check it.

What it does

  • Location search by postcode or place name, with street, satellite and topographic maps
  • Catchment drawing with automatic area calculation
  • M5-60 and r read from a digitised version of the Wallingford Procedure maps
  • Rainfall depth and intensity table from 5 minutes to 48 hours, with a climate change allowance
  • Rational Method peak runoff

Data sources

M5-60 and r values are digitised from The Wallingford Procedure, Volume 3 – Maps (HR Wallingford, 1981). Z1 and Z2 factors are from The Wallingford Procedure, Volume 4 – Modified Rational Method.
