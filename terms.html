1. Purpose

4. Methods and data

Rainfall is calculated with the Flood Studies Report method as set out in The Wallingford Procedure (HR Wallingford, 1981). M5-60 and r values are digitised from the Volume 3 maps and interpolated on a 2 km grid. Z1 values are digitised from the Volume 4 charts. FSR has been superseded by FEH for many applications; check which method your approving authority requires. No areal reduction factor is applied.
