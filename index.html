How is this calculated?

Rational Method:
Q = 2.78 × C × i × A

Q in L/s, C dimensionless, i in mm/hr, A in hectares.

FSR: M5-D = Z1 × M5-60; MT-D = Z2 × M5-D × (1 + CC); i = MT-D ÷ D. No areal reduction factor (fine for small catchments, under about 1 km²).
