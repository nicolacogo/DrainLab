How is this calculated?

Rational Method: Q = 2.78 × C × i × A (Q in L/s, i in mm/hr, A in ha).

FSR / Wallingford Procedure Vol 4:
M5-D = Z1 × M5-60
MT-D = Z2 × M5-D × (1 + CC)
i = MT-D ÷ D

Z1 from Fig A.3a/A.3b (digitised, interpolated in r and log D). Z2 from Tables A1/A2 (interpolated in M5-D and log T). M5-60 and r digitised from the Vol 3 maps. No areal reduction factor (suitable for small catchments).
